Eggdoku

One eggplant in every row, column and patch.

0:00

How to play

  • Place one eggplant in every row, every column and every color region.
  • Eggplants can't touch each other, not even diagonally.

Controls

  • Tap a square to mark or unmark it with ✕.
  • Double-tap to place an eggplant. Tap it again to remove it.
  • Drag from an empty square to mark ✕ on every square you cross. Start on a ✕ and drag to clear them.
  • Turn on Auto-✕ to mark the squares an eggplant rules out automatically.

Lives and score

  • An eggplant in the wrong square turns into a ✕ and costs a heart. Lose all three and the puzzle is over.
  • Bigger boards are worth more. Solve fast for a time bonus. Each mistake costs a quarter of the board's points.
